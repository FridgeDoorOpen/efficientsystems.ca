Every diagram on this site is drawn from the same small set of parts, so you learn them once and read them everywhere. The accent colour marks the point of a diagram and is used for nothing else.

There are two frames. The ISA-95 levels stack the operation from field devices up to the enterprise. The RAMI 4.0 grid adds a second axis across them, the architecture layers from asset to business, so that something like the information layer can be seen running through every level rather than sitting on top.
