Transformation teams are org units with incentives too. Measured on activity, such as initiatives launched, sprints completed, and tools adopted, they produce activity. When those measures aren't visible to the business, nobody can tell whether the activity improved anything.

A shared map fixes this. If every initiative has to point to a capability transition on the map, its value is legible to the people who run the assets, and change for its own sake becomes visible as exactly that. The same map shows experienced engineers where their knowledge is needed in the redesign.
