Principles

These are the principles I test everything else against. They're numbered for reference and versioned by hand, because they'll change as the thinking does. Each one names where it comes from.

  1. 01
    Architectures must be traversable by AI agents, and navigable by humans.

    If an agent can't walk the structure of a system, it can't reason about it. If a person can't find their way through it, they won't trust what the agent says.

    v1.0
  2. 02
    Simulate the asset; don't assume it.

    Business plans should rest on how the asset actually behaves, not on its nameplate.

    v1.0
  3. 03
    Incentives drive outcomes.

    Systems mirror the org chart that builds them, and the org chart mirrors what each executive is measured on.

    v1.0
  4. 04
    Know the fence before you move it.

    Do process archaeology before redesign. Find the decision a process serves before you change or automate it.

    v1.0
  5. 05
    Every change traces to a capability.

    No change for its own sake. Each initiative names the capability it moves, from what maturity to what, on which asset.

    v1.0
